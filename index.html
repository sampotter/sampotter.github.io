About me

I'm back in my hometown of Seattle, developing ridiculously fast and accurate numerical algorithms for Rhinoceros 3D's CAD kernel at Robert McNeel & Associates.

Previously I was a Research Scientist at Coreform (immersed isogeometric analysis) and a Courant Instructor at NYU with Leslie Greengard and Mike O'Neil, affiliated with the Flatiron Institute's Center for Computational Mathematics. I earned my PhD in computer science at Maryland, advised by Masha Cameron and Ramani Duraiswami.
